# Airsoft Days: ASD-39517-2024-7 vs ASD-39517-2025-7

What changed between the two editions of the Airsoft Days Taktikkarte, and what it
means for `src/scenarios/airsoft-days.ts`.

- **Old:** `asd_2024_taktikkarte.jpg`
- **New:** `asd_2025_taktikkarte.pdf`, fetched 11 September 2026 from
  `https://airsofthelden-events.com/airsoft-days/taktikkarte`, which redirects to
  `ASD2025_Taktikkarte_Web.pdf`

## How the two were compared

The editions ship at different sizes, so they were registered before anything was
read off them. Phase correlation over a scale sweep put the 2024 image onto the 2025
page at a scale of 1.775 with a 59 × 5 px offset. The two 100 m scale bars then
measured 151 px and 150 px, which is the independent check that the fit is right: at
that scale 1 px is 0.665 m.

Everything below is measured in that shared frame. The play areas were separated
from the apple-green surround by colour, and the boundaries compared by distance
transform.

## The substantive change: the MP faction is gone

The 2024 sheet drew building 800 as a headquarters — the legend's `○ Hauptquartier`
ring, in green — with the maroon winged **MP** emblem beside it. The 2025 sheet has
neither. Building 800 is now a plain numbered building like any other, and the map
carries two headquarters instead of three:

| Marker | 2024                      | 2025        |
| ------ | ------------------------- | ----------- |
| 714    | Delta Unit, red ring      | unchanged   |
| 820    | Ghost Infantry, gold ring | unchanged   |
| 800    | MP, green ring + emblem   | **removed** |

Cobra Gang still sits unringed at entrance Z2 in both, so it was never a
headquarters on this map.

## Legend: two renamed entries

Thirty-four of the thirty-six numbered entries are identical. Two changed, and they
swapped a term between them:

| Id  | 2024         | 2025           |
| --- | ------------ | -------------- |
| 631 | Niemandsland | Grabensystem   |
| 807 | Grabensystem | Schützengräben |

Read them together: what used to be called the trench system at 807 is now
`Schützengräben`, and the name `Grabensystem` moved to 631, which used to be
no-man's-land.

## Play area: redrawn by hand, same ground

| Measure                                  | Value    |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Median displacement of the 2025 boundary | 0.7 m    |
| 90th percentile                          | 6.6 m    |
| Maximum                                  | 11.0 m   |
| Enclosed area 2024                       | 102.8 ha |
| Enclosed area 2025                       | 101.8 ha |

A 1 ha net change across 103 ha, with no point of the boundary moving more than
about 11 m, is redrawing rather than a change on the ground. It sits well inside the
few tens of metres that `README.md` already claims for a traced boundary, and the
printed maps say the real edge is marked with tape anyway.

The north-east corner around 800 and 807 looks different at a glance, but the
boundary there is within a few metres of the old one. What changed the shape of that
corner to the eye is the missing green ring and MP emblem, not the line.

## Unchanged

Base satellite imagery, the A–H by 1–17 grid, the north arrow, the 100 m scale bar,
every building number and its position, the Cobra Gang marker at Z2, entrances Z1 to
Z4, the camping blocks A/B/C, the parking blocks, the Marktplatz and the Gelände
Zufahrt.

## What this means for `src/scenarios/airsoft-days.ts`

Not yet applied. Three edits follow from the above:

1. `"631": "Niemandsland"` becomes `"631": "Grabensystem"`.
2. `"807": "Grabensystem"` becomes `"807": "Schützengräben"`.
3. The `stuetzpunkt` entry in `headquarters` should go. It is the green 800 marker
   the 2025 map no longer draws, and it has no faction logo because there never was
   one in `public/logos/`.

The file's comment should also cite ASD-39517-2025-7 rather than ASD-39517-2024-7.
`playArea` needs no change; see the numbers above.

## Reading the new file

It is a one-page PDF from Illustrator, 841.89 × 546.78 pt, created 23 July 2025. The
labels are outlined, so there is no extractable text. The page carries one embedded
3000 × 2158 JPEG, which is the flattened artwork plus print bleed — the PDF page is
the trimmed version, so render the page rather than pulling the embedded raster:

```bash
python3 -c "import pymupdf; pymupdf.open('asd_2025_taktikkarte.pdf')[0].get_pixmap(dpi=260).save('asd2025.png')"
```

At 260 dpi that gives 3041 × 1975, which matches the embedded raster's detail without
resampling it twice.
